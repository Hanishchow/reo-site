What you can do

Everything a team chat needs.

Nothing you do not.

Text, voice notes, files

Hold to record a voice note. Send files up to 250 MB on the LAN and 100 MB anywhere else, hash-checked.

Calls, voice and video

Ring, accept, decline. Calls try the local network first, so on one campus the media never leaves the building.

Direct on your network

Two devices on the same Wi-Fi connect peer to peer. No round trip through the internet, no server in the middle.

SOS

SOS button

One red button alerts everyone who is online. Urgent bubbles stay on screen until someone taps "I'm on it".

Presence

See who is active now, online with the app closed, or out of reach. Anyone can hide their status.

SMS when there is no data

If someone is unreachable, send an SMS from your own phone. Every text asks first, because SMS costs money.

How a message travels

Closest path wins.

Reo tries the shortest route first and only falls back to the relay when it has to. Pick a situation.

For teams

Your company, on your own network.

Create a workspace, set who reports to whom, and tag a coworker with @name. Send official mail with a subject, To and CC. Add anyone in seconds by scanning their QR code or pasting their code.

Run the relay on a server you own and traffic stays inside the building. There is no per-seat price, and the 500th person costs the same as the fifth.

Privacy

The relay cannot read it.

Sealed on your device

Every message is encrypted with a key only you and the recipient hold, using X25519 and AES-256-GCM, and signed with Ed25519.

No accounts, no phone number

Your identity is a key pair made on your device. You add people by scanning a code in person.

Locked to your devices

Limit your identity to enrolled devices and known networks. A new device needs a one-time code from one you already trust.

Money stays with the bank

Pay requests hand off to UPI, *99# or your bank's SMS service. Reo never holds money or sees a PIN.
